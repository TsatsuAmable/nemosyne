The Runtime

Analysis with a sense of place

Nemosyne turns structured and streaming data into a spatial workspace. The runtime separates analytical operations from their visual embodiment so the same findings can be inspected through different views.

Quest hand tracking and controllers are supported directly. Desktop input provides a practical fallback for development, accessibility, and recovery.

Designed for

  • Exploration of complex relationships
  • World-space inspection and comparison
  • Embodied filtering and transformation
  • Reproducible analytical sessions

Capabilities

From data to understanding

A compact runtime for turning data, analysis, and spatial interaction into one inspectable workspace.

🎯

Spatial Data Mapping

Map CSV, JSON, and live-stream data into interactive 3D artefacts for embodied exploration and inspection.

🔮

Draco v1 Embodiment

A symbolic constraint engine selects layout, geometry, behaviour, and interaction from topology and statistical facts.

🤝

Collaborative Spaces

WebRTC/WebSocket foundations support shared memory palaces, peer presence, annotations, and guided tours.

⚡

WebXR Native

Runs directly on three.js and WebXR with Quest hand tracking, controllers, and a desktop recovery path.

🧩

Spatial Workspaces

Moveable HUD panels, a curved analyst dashboard, session persistence, and world-space inspection keep analysis legible.

📊

Analytical Operations

Filter, aggregate, sort, cluster, detect anomalies, inspect correlations, and expose TDA summaries in context.
