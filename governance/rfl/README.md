# Recursive Falsification Laboratory ledger

findings.jsonl is an append-only candidate-finding ledger produced by the RFL worker.

An empty ledger means the laboratory has not yet reported — begin discovery; it never means there is nothing to falsify. See `iteration-contract.md` for the iteration rules.

An RFL finding is not governance authority, a roadmap obligation, or proof that production is defective. It becomes actionable only after an independent owner reproduces and accepts it through the normal roadmap, issue, PR, or governance review-finding process.

The RFL worker may not mark its own finding accepted or closed.
