## 4. Structural finding — epistemic gate vs producer-label mismatch (admissibility mechanics, not a metric)

`FitnessModel`'s epistemic gates accept only `measured` or `derived` sources (`FitnessModel.ts:248-255/:374-381`) — but the canonical evidence producer labels exactly those facts `heuristic`: `hasClusters`/`separationScore`/`estimatedCount` (`DatasetEvidenceSignature.ts:261-271`), `highVariance` (`:236-242`) — and emits nothing for `depth` (`profile.rs:750`) or `densityVariation` (`:272-278`).

**Consequence:** under authority-clean canonical intake (`DatasetEvidence` → `datasetEvidenceToSignature`), the CLUSTER boost's `hasClusters` branch, the high-variance half of the DISTRIBUTION boost, the whole HIERARCHY boost and the F-5 `densityHandling` densityVariation term **cannot fire**; they fire only through the legacy `buildDatasetSignature`/`SignatureBuilder` path or historical persisted signatures. This inventory deliberately records those terms as *decision-bearing but currently unreachable under canonical intake* rather than as active ranking terms. Whether the gate or the labels are the thing to reconcile — and whether the boosts should ever have fired on `heuristic`-labelled facts — is a design decision above MA1's inventory remit; it is recorded here because every MA2 control for the affected families must be authored against that reality (a control that passes through the legacy path falsifies nothing on the canonical one). The labels themselves are correct per the TEC2 migration (heuristic terminology is preserved, not laundered) — the mismatch is between two independently correct artefacts, and resolving it is not "fix a test", it is deciding which of the two is wrong.

**(Corrected 2026-10-02 by the MA2 first slice — see `docs/audits/TEC3_MA2_CONTROL_HARNESS_2026-10-02.md` §1-§2, observed through the production model rather than inferred):** two halves of this finding were wrong. First, the legacy route for the CLUSTER boost and the high-variance DISTRIBUTION half does not exist in this build — `SignatureBuilder` deliberately leaves `clusterStructure` empty and never populates `highVariance`, so those terms fire through **no intake path**; only historical persisted signatures (or a manual epistemic flip) can reach them. Second, the two halves of the DISTRIBUTION boost are **not** under one uniform epistemic policy: the `hasOutliers` half's branch is ungated and *does* fire on the canonical producer's `heuristic` label (D = 0.35 through the same intake the CLUSTER branch refuses at D = 0.0) — recorded as gate asymmetry, and additional reason the boost ladder cannot be treated as calibrated. Also corrected by the same slice: §9 item 5's "no production-path falsifier at all" for the anomaly family — the MA2 first slice supplies kernel-side planted/clean/degenerate-floor controls and the TS readback test; and §3 K-8's floor prediction ("0.2 floor whenever any outlier exists") is **false** as a general rule — planted extreme outliers saturate the clamp (√24/5 ≈ 0.98); the floor binds only through the degenerate constant-column regime (std below the `1e-9` guard).

## 9. Metrics currently lacking a defensible calibration experiment (ordered by decision leverage)
