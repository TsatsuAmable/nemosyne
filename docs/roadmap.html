Roadmap to a qualified research preview

Product-transition exits PT0-PT8 and the minimum dataset-embodiment lane (FM-DSE, DSE6 qualified) are landed, and review findings RF-037 through RF-043 are verified complete. Full Moneta is implementation-partial: the direct critique-to-alternative loop, lineage capture and headless projections are landed, and investigator-visible qualification plus physical-device evidence remain open. Feature count does not substitute for evidence that the full investigation works safely on its target hardware.

Next implementation order

  1. Decide FM8 evidence binding. Accept or amend proposed RFC 0013 so each composed representation binds to semantically compatible governed evidence.
  2. Qualify investigator-visible surfaces. Exercise the FM1/FM2 perspective and alternative surfaces through the real product path, and measure FM5 advisory value.
  3. Qualify the device boundary. Run the instrumented interaction and endurance protocols on physical Meta Quest 3S hardware, including UXR4/UXR5 envelopes.
  4. Measure spatial performance. Attribute production cost from device benchmarks before selecting a query accelerator; remove redundant paths only on measured evidence.

Private preview gate

P1 can reopen only after blocker and high-severity audit findings have governed dispositions, the full CI gate remains green, and target-device evidence is reviewed. Learned ranking and future adaptive representation remain opt-in and must not bypass evidence policy.

Evidence vocabulary

  • Built: a component and tests exist.
  • Wired: production instantiates the component.
  • Qualified: the declared environment and protocol pass.
  • Validated: research evidence supports the intended human outcome.
