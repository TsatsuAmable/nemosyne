## Adversarial review

**Evidence/statistical attack.** These tests establish deterministic software contracts, not empirical claims about physical memory, latency, comfort, investigator performance, or discovery value. Source-N independence means semantic/presentation cardinality is contract-bounded or fail-closed; it does not mean runtime cost is independent of N.

**UX/spatial attack.** Progressive materialisation retains parent semantic structure under detail admission/refusal and preserves durable identity across eviction/reconstruction. This is structural legibility evidence only; human legibility remains unqualified until appropriate human evidence.

**Dataset-first attack.** Aggregate, Distribution, Density, Cluster and Relationship Graph operate on semantic summaries/source-authoritative structures rather than falling back to a raw point cloud. Distribution summary marks explicitly refuse member-container drill-down where membership semantics are not established.

**Authority attack.** Rust/WASM remains analytical authority. Presentation materialisation limits cannot upgrade analytical truth, and malformed/widened requests fail closed.
