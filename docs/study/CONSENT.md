# Participant consent

Status: Draft. This is a study-design framework and not a substitute for institutional ethics or IRB review.

## What this study involves
Participants will complete a data-analysis task under one of three conditions: a standard 2D condition, a desktop 3D condition, or a VR condition.

## What is recorded
The exact recorded fields must match the final data dictionary. In general, the study may record:
- task answer and correctness
- response time and completion metrics
- confidence and workload self-report
- condition assignment
- participant identifier
- optional structured observer notes and intervention logs
- head and hand movement only if the VR condition explicitly requires it and the participant is informed

## What is not recorded
The study should state clearly which raw streams are not captured, such as raw gaze traces, permanent video capture, or other unnecessary personal data, unless they are explicitly approved and disclosed.

## Identification
Participants are assigned a pseudonymous study ID. No direct personal identifiers should be stored in the study dataset unless required by the institution and disclosed in advance.

## Retention and withdrawal
The study should define a retention period and a withdrawal pathway. Data deletion or export support should be a real operational path, not just a policy statement.

## Questions or concerns
Provide contact information for the study lead and any oversight body.
