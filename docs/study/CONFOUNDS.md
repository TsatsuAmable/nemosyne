# Confounds register

Status: Draft study-governance artifact.

## 1. Condition order
Confound: order effects from performing one condition before another.
Control: counterbalanced assignment across participants.
Measurement: order recorded as a covariate.
Residual risk: moderate.

## 2. Learning and carry-over
Confound: task familiarity or strategy transfer between conditions.
Control: equivalent-but-non-identical task instances and training before both conditions.
Measurement: task-instance difficulty match and trial-order trend logs.
Residual risk: moderate.

## 3. Training asymmetry
Confound: one condition receives better instruction or more practice.
Control: equivalent training duration and script parity.
Measurement: training duration and script version.
Residual risk: low if parity is verified.

## 4. VR novelty
Confound: novelty or discomfort of VR inflates the interface effect.
Control: record prior VR familiarity and include a non-task familiarization period where appropriate.
Measurement: questionnaire items and comfort metrics.
Residual risk: moderate.

## 5. Hardware latency or stability
Confound: technical issues produce performance degradation unrelated to the representation itself.
Control: hardware gate and live performance checks before the study is run.
Measurement: frame rate, latency, and protocol-state telemetry.
Residual risk: low if gate criteria are enforced.

## 6. Researcher intervention
Confound: inconsistent support or prompts across conditions.
Control: fixed intervention policy and structured logs.
Measurement: observer event logs.
Residual risk: low if all intervention actions are recorded.

## 7. Information asymmetry
Confound: one condition exposes more information implicitly than the other.
Control: explicit information checklist and interface audit.
Measurement: manual audit of available information across condition versions.
Residual risk: moderate.

## 8. Measurement asymmetry
Confound: different interfaces capture outcomes in different ways.
Control: common outcome definition and condition-agnostic capture method.
Measurement: response-time and completion logs reviewed for modality artifacts.
Residual risk: moderate.

## Governance note
Any item listed here is a potential threat to validity. The fact that a confound is recognized is not enough; it must have a documented control or a clear residual-risk statement.
