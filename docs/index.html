Nemosyne is an open research instrument for turning analytical evidence into investigations people can inspect, challenge, and replay across desktop and WebXR.

Alpha research software, not a validated instrument or commercial product. Meta Quest 3S device qualification is still pending.

The Runtime

Investigation with a sense of place

Nemosyne keeps analytical evidence, decisions, annotations, and session history together so a finding can be traced and reproduced instead of merely presented.

The same investigation model is available on desktop and through WebXR. Quest hand and controller paths are implemented, but physical-device performance and interaction qualification remain open work.

Designed for

  • Evidence-backed discovery
  • Hypotheses that can be challenged
  • Reproducible investigation sessions
  • Comparable desktop and WebXR workflows

Capabilities

From evidence to reproducible insight

Implemented foundations for inspecting analytical evidence, recording decisions, and replaying how an investigation reached its conclusions.

🎯

Rust/WASM Evidence

The Rust/WASM kernel is the sole analytical authority. If it is unavailable, Nemosyne reports that state instead of quietly changing methods.

🔮

Moneta Representation

Moneta selects bounded representations from kernel evidence and records its reasoning, or abstains when the evidence is insufficient.

🤝

Investigation Provenance

Decisions, annotations, transforms, and evidence references travel with the investigation so findings can be inspected and challenged.

⚡

Desktop + WebXR

Desktop, controller, and hand-input foundations share one investigation model, with physical Quest qualification explicitly pending.

🧩

Portable Replay

Versioned Nemosyne packages preserve datasets, investigation state, provenance, and integrity checks for controlled replay.

📊

Research Instrumentation

Telemetry, study protocols, accessibility foundations, and explicit qualification gates support evaluation without overstating readiness.
